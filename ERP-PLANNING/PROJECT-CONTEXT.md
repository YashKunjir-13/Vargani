# Pauti Pustak Product Context

## Product purpose

Pauti Pustak is a multi-tenant financial and operational management platform for religious, social, and community events. It replaces paper registers, spreadsheets, disconnected payment records, and ad-hoc messaging with controlled contributor, collection, receipt, expense, volunteer, and reporting workflows.

## Primary users

- Platform Super Administrator
- Organization Owner
- President and Secretary
- Treasurer
- Volunteer Coordinator
- Collector
- Expense Approver
- Auditor
- Contributor
- Public Visitor

## Product modules

1. Authentication and sessions
2. Organizations, memberships, roles, and permissions
3. Events and event lifecycle
4. Volunteers and assignments
5. Contributor accounts and duplicate management
6. Personalized contribution bills and batches
7. Payment collection and verification
8. Unified contributions
9. Contribution receipts
10. Expenses, approvals, and payments
11. Financial accounts, transfers, and ledger
12. Reports, exports, dashboards, and public transparency
13. Notifications and communications
14. Documents and private file storage
15. Audit records and platform administration

## Core business goals

- Reduce financial and reconciliation errors.
- Preserve complete, immutable financial history.
- Provide trustworthy multilingual bills and receipts.
- Improve collector and volunteer accountability.
- Give organization leadership real-time event visibility.
- Support multiple independent organizations without data leakage.
- Provide a reusable SaaS foundation for community organizations.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Money is stored as integer paise, never floating point.
- Every protected record is tenant-scoped.
- Client-side permission checks never replace backend authorization.
- Confirmed financial records are not deleted or silently modified.
- Payment confirmation is backend-controlled and idempotent.
- Official bills and receipts are generated by the backend.
- Every important state change creates an audit event.
- Retried commands, jobs, and webhooks must not duplicate financial effects.

## Technology baseline

- Client: Flutter + Dart, Material 3, Riverpod, go_router, Dio, Freezed/json_serializable, Drift, secure platform storage, English/Marathi/Hindi localization.
- Backend: NestJS 11 + TypeScript, PostgreSQL + Prisma, Redis + BullMQ, NATS JetStream, REST/OpenAPI, gRPC, S3-compatible private storage, Handlebars/Puppeteer PDF generation, Razorpay.
- Platform: Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, GitLab CI, OpenTelemetry, structured logs, metrics, traces, alerts, runbooks.
- Quality: Jest, Supertest, Testcontainers, Pact/contract tests, Playwright, Flutter tests, integration tests, k6, SAST/DAST/dependency scanning.
