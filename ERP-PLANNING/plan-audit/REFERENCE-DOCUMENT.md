# Phase 3 Reference — Plan Audit

A plan audit is an independent challenge process, not a summary of the plan.

## Review dimensions

- Business alignment
- Requirement completeness and traceability
- Architectural correctness
- Domain boundaries and data ownership
- Security and privacy
- Multi-tenant isolation
- Financial integrity and idempotency
- Scalability, performance, availability, and recovery
- Operational feasibility
- Team skill, cost, schedule, and complexity
- Testability, observability, migration, and rollback

## Finding severity

- Critical: unacceptable financial, security, legal, or delivery failure; blocks approval.
- High: material risk requiring an approved remediation before dependent work.
- Medium: important improvement with owner and target date.
- Low: non-blocking quality or documentation improvement.
- Observation: recommendation or future consideration.

## Audit rule

The audit must cite exact plan sections and must not mark an item compliant merely because a heading exists.
