# Software Idea Input Questionnaire

1. What real-world problem is being solved?
2. Who experiences the problem most frequently?
3. How is the work performed today?
4. What errors, delays, costs, or trust problems occur?
5. Which users create, approve, verify, view, or audit records?
6. What must the MVP accomplish on day one?
7. What is explicitly outside the MVP?
8. What measurable outcome defines success?
9. What legal, payment, language, offline, or device constraints exist?
10. Which integrations are mandatory, optional, or future?
11. What data is sensitive or legally significant?
12. Which workflows must continue when a provider or network is unavailable?
